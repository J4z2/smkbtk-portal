### Privacy Notice: SMK Bandar Tasik Kesuma Student Performance Portal

**1. Who we are.** This portal is operated by SMK Bandar Tasik Kesuma ("the school"), [school address]. Questions about personal data can be sent to [officer name/title], [email/phone].

**2. Data we keep.**
- Student details: name, student number, MyKid/IC number, date of birth, gender, class, passport photo, marks, grades, attendance, co-curricular activities, teacher comments and intervention records.
- Statistical details the school is required to keep: race, religion and citizenship status.
- Parent/guardian details: name, IC number, phone number, email, address and relationship to the student.
- Portal usage records: login times, IP address and actions taken.

**3. Purpose.** The data is used to record and report students' academic progress and attendance, communicate with parents, plan support and interventions, and run the school. It is not used for marketing and is never sold.

**4. Who can see the data.**
- Parents/guardians see only their own children, and only reports that the school has approved and published.
- Class teachers see the students in their own class.
- Appointed school administrators see data for the whole school.
- Race, religion, citizenship and IC numbers are shown only to administrators and the student's class teacher.
- Data may be shared with the Ministry of Education, the State Education Department or authorities where the law requires it.

**5. Storage and security.** Data is stored on servers in Malaysia. Passwords are stored hashed, IC numbers are encrypted, and every change is recorded in an audit log.

**6. How long we keep it.** Student records are kept while the student is at the school and for [5] years after they leave. After that they are deleted or anonymised, unless the law requires them to be kept longer.

**7. Your rights.** You may ask to see or correct your data and your child's data by contacting the school office. Change requests are handled by the school administrators.

**8. Consent.** By clicking "I agree", you confirm that you have read this notice and agree to your data being processed as described.

**9. Changes.** If this notice is updated, you will be asked to read and accept the new version before continuing to use the portal.
